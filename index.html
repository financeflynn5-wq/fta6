You are Rocco. Steal a ride. Grab a gun. Lose the cops. Keep the cash.

Earn: steal the car Scrap orders and park it on the pink marker at his chop shop, or drive a taxi and pick up fares at the gold markers.

Explore: races, stunt jumps, a pizza run, speed cameras, car bowling, bounty hunts, a demolition derby, a gun range, a mall smash spree and 40 golden tins are spread over the hills, the beach, the port and the desert. North over the hills is the valley: a mall you can drive through, a big park, a marina, farmland and an army base. Boats wait at the marina, the beach pier and the port; helicopters sit on the pads marked H. Hold Tab for the map.
